# Cutiva for macOS

Edit videos by telling Claude what you want. Cutiva runs locally on your Mac and connects to Claude Desktop.

## Download

**[Download Cutiva.dmg](https://github.com/fbk93/cutiva-releases/releases/latest/download/Cutiva.dmg)**
(macOS 12 or later, Mac with Apple chip – M1 or newer)

1. Open `Cutiva.dmg` and drag Cutiva into your Applications folder.
2. Start Cutiva. It is signed and notarized by Apple and opens with a double click.
3. The setup window walks you through your licence, connecting Claude Desktop and getting started.

**Try it:** choose "Try free for 7 days" in the app.
**Buy:** one-time purchase incl. VAT, no subscription. Future updates included, up to two of your own Macs.
In the app via "Buy a license", or **[here](https://getcutiva.com)**.

Requires Claude Desktop and your own Anthropic account.

Updates arrive automatically (menu bar → Cutiva → Updates). Coming from 1.0.0 or 1.0.1: download `Cutiva.dmg` once
and replace the app in Applications; from 1.0.2 on, updates install themselves.
